Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NovaApplications ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the TimberCalc mobile application (the "App").

Summary

  • The App is a timber volume calculator tool designed for personal and professional use.
  • We do not collect personal identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address).
  • All calculations and calculation history are stored strictly locally on your device and are never uploaded to any external server.
  • We display advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Google AdMob may collect the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) and device information to serve and measure ads.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not require user registration or accounts, and does not collect any personal information.

All timber volume calculations, piece counts, and calculation history records are stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device. This data never leaves your device.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements within the App.

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Android Advertising ID (AD_ID)
  • Device identifiers, device model, and operating system version
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics

To learn more about how Google collects and uses information for advertising, please refer to:
Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

GDPR and Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, advertisements served via Google AdMob comply with applicable European privacy regulations and Google's EU User Consent Policy.

Children's Privacy

The App is a utility tool intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store user data on remote servers, no user data is retained by NovaApplications. You can delete your local calculation history at any time directly within the App or by clearing the App's storage in your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
